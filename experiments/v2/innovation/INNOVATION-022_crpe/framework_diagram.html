可靠性来源

local_c = Norm(mean(first_6_sentences_c))
residual_c = local_c - proj_class_name_c(local_c)
strength_c = ||residual_c||
reliability_c = clip((strength_c - seen_mean)/(2×seen_std), -1, 1)

reliability只由文本和150个seen类的统计中心/尺度产生；unseen类文本允许使用，unseen图像不参与。

推理与训练

beta_c = fixed_CCPE_beta + delta × reliability_c
final_logits_c = SEBC_logits_c + beta_c × top2_patch_score_c
delta = 2 × tanh(raw_delta)
