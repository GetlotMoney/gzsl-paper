推理结构

推理与CASR相同：固定OESR beta，使用完整8句全局softmax权重，无新增门控。

训练期dropout

full_w = softmax(log(CASR_w) + 0.5 × tanh(residual))
train_w = renormalize(full_w after masking one random sentence)
loss = seen CE(train_w) + 0.01 × KL(full_w || CASR_w)

残差零初始化，推理初始严格复现CASR。每批恰好mask一句，mask计数写入checkpoint。
