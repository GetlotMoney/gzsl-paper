类别条件权重

agreement_c = standardized cos(Claude_text_c, merge_text_c)
w_c = sigmoid(bias + slope × agreement_c)
final_logits_c = SEBC_logits_c
               + w_c × beta_claude × claude_logits_c
               + (1-w_c) × beta_merge × merge_logits_c

bias/slope零初始化，全部类别初始w=0.5；只训练两个共享参数，端点beta固定。
